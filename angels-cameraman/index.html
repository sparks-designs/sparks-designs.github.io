Angel's Cameraman

Turns a 360° tracking stand into a smooth camera operator that follows a dancer, spins and all.

Download for Android

Always the latest version · all versions & what's new

Installing

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (this is normal for apps not from the Play Store).
  3. Open Angel's Cameraman and allow camera, microphone and nearby devices (for the stand's Bluetooth).
  4. Switch the stand on. The status box shows Stand: connected when it's ready.

Updating: download and install again. Your settings are kept.

First time: calibrate the stand

Open ⚙ → Stand → Calibrate stand, point the camera at the room (no one needs to be in shot) and leave it for about a minute. The stand turns both ways while the app learns how it moves. You only do this once per stand.

Using it

  • Tap the dancer on the screen to choose who to follow.
  • Track / Off: start or stop following.
  • ● Rec: record video to Movies/Angels Cameraman. Nothing drawn on screen is ever recorded.
  • ⟲ Cam: switch between front and back camera.
  • ◀ ▶: hold to turn the stand by hand.
  • ⚙: Auto suits most people. Use Wide with the back camera to keep a moving dancer in view.

Works with the white "Eaipai" / TrackAI style 360° auto-tracking phone stands. Needs Android 8 or newer.
